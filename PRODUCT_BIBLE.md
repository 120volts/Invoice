# Product Bible

## Mission

Financial Brain should reduce bookkeeping effort to nearly zero.

## Workflow

Capture -\> Invoice -\> Payment -\> Dashboard -\> Taxes

## Product Decisions

-   Fast invoice creation
-   Saved clients and services
-   Mileage support
-   Job costs
-   Equipment rental
-   Invoice statuses:
    -   Draft (yellow)
    -   Sent
    -   Viewed
    -   Awaiting Payment
    -   Overdue (red)
    -   Paid (green)
-   PDF generation
-   Spanish PDFs
-   Dashboard cards navigate to filtered views
-   Future integration with Purchases, Projects, Clients and Taxes
